# What the user sees

What their page does at each step. SKILL.md holds what you do; read this when the user asks how the page behaves, or you need to explain it.

## Your cursor (step 2's `--agent`)

A cursor carrying your name and logo does your part on their page. Each of its clicks sends out two circles, one inside the other. It starts each round's page (step 3). The first time the user looks at the page, it works down their notes column, top to bottom, doing each thing still in view when it gets there (its bubble showing is enough): it types each of your messages where it goes, as a one-line typing bubble that grows into your words, and closes each thread the user answered in chat (step 7). A message that the ones above it push out of view as they grow in gets no visit. As the cursor leaves, everything else is there at once, on screen or further down. If the user scrolls, clicks or presses a key while it works, everything is there at once and the cursor fades. It also clicks in the states you cause on their notes bar: Received and Updating when their paste arrives (step 4), where it stays parked while you work, and Done (step 8). If the user wasn't looking when a state came, it clicks in only the state the bar has reached: Updating once Received's beat is over, Done when that's waiting, never the steps the user missed.

With helpers (step 2's `--helper` and `--owns`): on the page, each agent does only what it owns: a helper wipes in its own parts in its own colour and turns the round number, and you wipe the Outline and the rest and type your messages.

## Each round's opening (step 3)

The opening waits for the first time the user visits the round, even if the agent prepared it hours earlier. It counts as seen once it finishes or the user skips it by interacting with the page. Re-running notes or reloading a completed round does not play it again. Reloading an unfinished opening lets it play again. A new round gets its own opening.

The previous page and its Outline load before the tab has focus. Loading placeholders fill both areas until that snapshot is ready. The opening starts when the tab is visible and the browser window has focus. Switching away pauses the round-number change and wipes until the user returns. It shows as the user left it: on round one a quiet page skeleton and Outline placeholders, on a later round the version before it, with their notes bar as the user left it: dark, reading the round before's number and Updating (green and Done if you'd called it done). Your cursor flies in and clicks the top left of the page's words. On round one the loading shimmer stops as the cursor arrives; the same downward wipe removes the skeleton as the words appear. The Outline and page wipe down together to the new version, leaving the Outline's title bar untouched. Then the cursor clicks the round number to begin the turn: the bar fades back to its light ground and the number rolls up to this round's. Then the cursor types each of your messages in view into their notes column (closed notes stay in their earlier round, with no stamping animation), top to bottom, and the rest are there as it leaves. The cursor moves on as soon as the shared wipe passes the last words in both columns. With helpers (step 2), each agent has its own cursor and does only what it owns: your click in the page starts the Outline and your parts of the page together, each helper wipes its own parts at its own moment (two can wipe at once), a helper then clicks the round number, and you type the messages. The page click starts the wipes, all visible parts finish before the round turns, and the round turns before the notes; otherwise each comes and goes on its own. The round number and arrows sit in the Notes header and stay in place as the number rolls. On an earlier round, a double chevron right after Next jumps to the latest round. On the latest round, a Replay icon sits halfway between Next and the Notes toggle until the user copies; a copy or Done hides it. Received, Updating and Done take the same spot; a copy alone shows nothing there, since the Copy button already says it. Hover over, focus or tap the round label to see the date for that round. Replay wipes the Outline and page back up together and plays the opening again. It is absent under reduced motion.

## Edit and Highlight in framed mockups

Select words inside a local mockup to use the same menu as on the review page. Edit opens the words in place and shows what changed when you finish. Highlight marks words without starting a thread. The review page saves both, and ⌘Z takes back the last change. Copied edits name the mockup file and include the original and replacement wording.

## Threads and navigation

Pointing at the final agent message bubble opens its reaction picker after the same short pause as pointing at its sticker circle. The picker still grows from the circle, then settles above or below the message so its words remain readable and selectable. The quoted content beneath a note’s reaction does the same. Opening directly from the circle keeps its existing position. Earlier message bubbles and blue authored comments and replies do not open a picker. Moving between that content, its circle, and the picker keeps it open; leaving them closes it after the usual grace period. The source thread stays fully opaque while its picker is open.

The notes column shows threads and change notices. An agent question or reply stays open while it waits for the user. A change notice opens a thread when the user asks for an agent reply. The user’s other notes and edits stay outside the open count. A committed reply, reaction, or picture closes a question waiting for the user. A question also closes when the user leaves a note, reaction, or picture on its part (SKILL.md step 7). Its dot goes and its Reply box stays. Deleting those notes opens it again. Drafts stay open on unanswered questions. New labels parts added in response to notes.

Small type and part headings are hidden. Each open thread has a purple dot at the upper right of its thread-number row (10 px across before the 1.11 scale is applied). It does not take space from the quote or message bubbles. Closing the thread bursts that dot out over 380 ms. Removing its last response pops it back in over 360 ms. Undo reverses the change. Under reduced motion, each state changes at once.

Outline badges show all threads for each part, with a purple dot when any are open. Both collapsed columns show the total thread count with the same any-open dot. Answering a thread changes its open state without reducing its total.

The expanded notes bar puts the thread arrows and total count on the left. On the right, a purple dot follows a nonzero open count with a 10 px CSS gap. At zero, `0 open` has no dot or reserved gap. Closing the last thread pops its dot away, then slides the count right over 320 ms after a 130 ms head start for the pop. Reopening slides it left and pops the dot back in. An empty page starts compact, and reduced motion changes both states immediately. Clicking the legend goes to the first open thread; it is disabled at zero. The expanded total reads `N threads`, and the selected row is numbered `i of N`. Collapsed, the seeker reads `i/N`, or `–/N` with none selected. Its arrows always visit every thread in page order and wrap, including when none are open. ⌥↑ / ⌥↓ do the same on the page or in a thread's Reply box.

Automatic advance visits only threads waiting for the user. Committing a reply or reacting to an agent message moves to the next thread waiting for the user, including across tabs. The new panel and notes fade in together when the tab changes. Past the last thread down the page it wraps to the first one still waiting above. When there are none left it stops and focuses Overall feedback. A draft does not advance. Reduced motion makes the move instant.

## Title row and tooltips

Once the page's first heading scrolls out of view, its words fade in at the top of the page's column. The row is as tall as the Outline's first row. Its words use the `/human-feedback` name's font, size and letter spacing and sit on the same baseline, lined up with the page's text. A long title ends in an ellipsis. Pointing at that title, or tabbing to it, shows the full title in heavier type above the file path. A shortened title without a file path still shows its full name. The tooltip stays open when you move the pointer onto it; Escape dismisses it. A title that fits keeps the usual path-only tooltip. A page with no heading, or a window too small for the columns, gets no title row.

Every tooltip shows as soon as the pointer or keyboard reaches its control, with a quick fade. A file path can widen the tooltip and breaks only after a slash. A tooltip that wraps is only as wide as its longest line.

## Tabs

On a page with `role="tab"` tabs, one panel shows at a time. The tab bar the layer draws sticks to the top of the window, just under the layer's title bar whenever the title bar is showing. With tabs inside a tab, the inner tab bar sticks under the outer tab bar. The Outline lists every tab, open or closed, as a level of its hierarchy: tabs at the top of the page are its top level, tabs in a section are that section's children, a tab inside a tab sits one level deeper, and what's in a tab is inset under its name. A click on a tab or on anything under it opens the tab. A thread on a part in a closed tab waits, unseen, and still counts: the arrows go through all threads in page order and open each tab they reach. Answering a thread opens another tab if it holds the next open thread. The open tab stays through a reload and a step between versions, in that browser tab. On a phone, opening the sheet with threads waiting only in closed tabs goes to the first of them.

The Overall feedback box shows all its words only while the cursor is in it. Out of it, it folds back to two lines with a short animation (none with reduced motion), so a long overall note never covers a thread.

## Reactions and narrow windows

In the notes column, reactions to the document hang off the bottom right corner of the quote, picture or part name. The sticker and its picker stay there whether the comment is empty, being typed or saved. Reactions to an agent message sit on that message's bottom right corner and never appear on the document. The done check on the left keeps the same vertical overlap as the reaction on the right. Document reactions use an empty section gutter, trying the right side then the left; they stay hidden when neither side fits.

When the notes column opens as a narrow overlay, the covered document scrollbar and connector lines hide. The left rail stays visible when it fits. In any window too small to show the page with both columns open (under about 1,108 px wide) or under 480 px tall, both columns go: the page fills the window, and a round button at the bottom right opens the notes as a sheet, with a dot while questions wait. The Outline and the Top and Bottom controls aren't shown there.

## When their paste arrives (step 4's `--received`)

Copy locks the whole round, including unanswered threads. Reply boxes disappear, and replies, reactions, and attachments wait for Unlock. Reloading keeps the round locked. Unanswered threads keep their open dots and counts. The agent's name bubble stays attached below-right, including while Updating. It can extend past the window edge.

What the user copied is already read-only from the moment the user copied it, each bubble the user sent kept as it was with a tick in its corner, two once this has run; within a few seconds of this run, your cursor flies in and clicks their notes bar, which turns dark and reads Received beside the round's number. After a beat it clicks again for Updating and stays parked beside its spinner, with a gap between the point and the spinning circle, while you work (at most 30 minutes). Under reduced motion, or with their notes column shut, the states just appear. The user can still copy it again, or unlock it to keep editing or add notes.

## Replies and done marks (step 7)

When a note asks a question, the user sees their note in Notes, your answer under it, and a Reply box to answer back once. Longer explanations can sit in the page, linked from the reply. With `--gist '<note as copied>' "<short version>"`, their note shows its short version, in their own words, with See original underneath. That expands to all their words in place; See summary folds it back. This works on carried replies too; without a gist their note looks as before. Copy keeps their full words, even when folded. Gists carry with their thread. A run with `--gist` replaces the page's gists; a plain re-run keeps them.

Your answer is short. A blank line in `--reply`, `--again` or `--ask` makes separate bubbles in a pale wash of your violet, stacked in order; one paragraph stays one bubble. Links work in each, and React and the sticker sit on the last. The opening types in one bubble, then shows the paragraphs together. Copy joins your reply's paragraphs with spaces. The user's own notes and answers split the same way once saved or sent: a blank line in his words shows as one blue bubble a paragraph, on every round and earlier version, in a gist's See original, and on Overall from the round before. The receipt or done mark sits on his last bubble. His Comment and Reply boxes stay one box while he types, a click on any bubble opens the whole note in that box, and Copy keeps his words exactly as written.

The row counts as one thread and stays open until the user answers or the agent closes it. It gets no New pill, and Copy never reminds the user to answer it. A done mark marks the original note in the round where the user wrote it. When the user steps back with the version arrows, that row has the ✅, stays read-only and counts as a thread that isn't open. The new round has no duplicate row for it, and the opening never stamps it. A thread the user closes with an emoji reaction stays in its own round too, with the reaction, and isn't carried. The new round holds your replies and questions and carried unanswered threads. Done marks add no separate thread or count.

## Changed and added parts (step 7's `--updated` and `--added`)

An `--updated` notice shows Updated beside the existing words that changed. An `--added` notice shows New on a part that was added. New takes precedence for notices on that part. Select a notice to reveal Reply, or use React on its bubble. At first the notice is outside the thread count, arrows, and Copy reminder. A ❓ reaction or a committed written question adds it to the thread list and marks it Awaiting reply. A question mark inside a link or quoted text stays ordinary feedback. The user can still copy the feedback immediately. The notice and its pill stay with their round. Responses copy under Your updates, where the agent reads the context and answers anything that needs a reply.

## Done (step 8)

Under Done the summary heads the notes column, and every thread of the round follows it in page order, in one list that scrolls between the bar and Unlock. The threads are read-only there and draw no connector line; Unlock puts them back beside their words, with their Reply boxes.

## Message dates

A centred date groups messages within each thread, appearing again when the day changes. The current day reads Today; hover or keyboard focus shows the full date, time, and time zone. Round labels use that same full format, with “at” between the date and time. Current agent messages use a stronger grey than carried messages, and all questions and replies use normal-weight text. Dates stay fully visible when another thread is selected. A user message’s date is when the agent acknowledges its paste, not when the user typed it. Older messages whose receipt time cannot be recovered show no date.
